Kob khun means thank you. This is where we keep what families tell us, and where we celebrate our children and what they go on to do.

July 2026 · Celebrating our children

Congratulations, Pari.

Panitbhand Paribatra Na Ayudhya, Pari to us, is one of our Year 6 alumni. At fourteen he is an underwater photographer, and this month his series Dwellers of the Night won the Project//21 category at the Hasselblad Masters, chosen from more than 108,000 entries worldwide, a year after he was named Young Shark Photographer of the Year. Asked how it began, he told BK Magazine: "I practiced and practiced and it became a passion." A child who finds the thing that fascinates them and keeps going is what we hope for every child here.
