Nuts and bolts

Lunch at The City School.

The school provides a snack each day. Lunch is not included, so families either send lunch in or order a lunch box from one of two providers who deliver to school. Both are optional, and you order from them directly.

How lunch works
Snack is provided, lunch is not
Every child gets a balanced snack from the school each day. Lunch is up to your family: send it in, or order a lunch box.
Boxes arrive before lunchtime
Lunch boxes are delivered in the late morning and reach every child before their lunchtime. If you are dropping a lunch at school yourself, the office confirms the time.
Label everything you send in
Put your child's name and class on the lunch box and the bottle, especially if you are dropping lunch at school later in the morning.
The City School only
Both providers deliver to The City School. Lunch at the Purple Elephant centres works differently: ask the centre directly.

Getfresh · order by the item
Order what you want, day by day
Getfresh cook with natural and organic ingredients and sell by the item rather than by the week.
Order on Line at @getfreshth
Order at least one day ahead, before 15:00. ELC families get 15% off with the code GFELC15.
The kind of thing that arrives
Ham and cheese panini, margherita panini, New Orleans chicken wrap, spaghetti carbonara, mushroom ravioli, tuna melt baguette, grilled chicken caesar salad, potato and leek soup with multigrain toast. The margherita panini and the mushroom ravioli are vegetarian.

Whichever you order from
The order form routes the box
The provider's order form asks for the details that get the box to the right child at lunchtime. Fill it in fully and the box finds them.
Anything about a delivery goes to the provider
You order and pay the provider directly, so a missing or late box is theirs to fix. If a box does not arrive and your child needs lunch, the office will sort something out on the day: .

Prices, menus and ordering details come from the providers and can change. If something here does not match what they tell you, they are right and we will fix this page: use the feedback button at the bottom of any page.
